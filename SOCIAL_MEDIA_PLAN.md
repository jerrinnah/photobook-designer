## 1. Strategy in one paragraph

We meet professional photographers where they already live — primarily **Instagram, TikTok, and WhatsApp** — with content that solves their actual workflow problems. Posts are educational first, promotional second. Every piece of content reinforces the brand promise (speed, fair pricing, no subscriptions, professional output) and ladders up to OctaveDev's reputation for building polished products. Conversion happens off-platform, on the landing page.

## 3. Content pillars (ratio: 50/30/20)

### Pillar 1 (50%): Workflow education
*"How working photographers actually deliver albums faster."*

- "5 InDesign mistakes that cost you hours per album"
- "Why your client takes 2 weeks to approve a book — and how to fix it"
- "CMYK vs RGB: print-ready files explained in 60 seconds"
- "Pricing your wedding albums: 3 frameworks that work"
- Generic photographer workflow tips that *happen* to be solved by AutoBook

### Pillar 2 (30%): Product proof
*"AutoBook in action — real albums, real time."*

- Time-lapsed: design a 20-spread album in 5 minutes (real screen recording)
- Before/after: 30-photo dump → polished book
- Feature spotlights (Reshuffle, Client Proofing, Auto Design)
- New template drops with sample books

### Pillar 3 (20%): Community + behind-the-scenes
*"The team behind AutoBook."*

- OctaveDev studio updates (cross-promote parent brand)
- Photographer customer features ("This week's book by @studio")
- Process posts ("Here's how we built per-spread feedback")
- Founder PSA / industry takes

```

        - Tweak 2-3 spreads
```

- Reply to every comment on owned posts within 4 hours

## 8. Influencer / creator partnerships

### Tier 1 — Macro (10K+ photographer followers)
**Target**: 2-3 partnerships per quarter
**Offering**: free Pro license, ₦100K honorarium, custom referral code
**Deliverables**: 1 dedicated review video + 2 Instagram posts + ongoing affiliate link

### Tier 2 — Micro (1K-10K followers)
**Target**: 5-10 partnerships per quarter
**Offering**: free Pro license + 30% affiliate commission on referred sales
**Deliverables**: 1 review post, mention in album-delivery posts going forward

### Tier 3 — Nano (<1K followers, high engagement)
**Target**: 20+ ambassadors
**Offering**: free Pro license + 50% commission on direct referrals
**Deliverables**: organic mentions when relevant; no required posts

### Where to find them
- Search Instagram + TikTok for #weddingphotography in Nigeria/Ghana/Kenya/SA
- Look for photographers with 1K-50K followers who post BTS / educational content
- Filter for those whose audience engages (not just lurkers)

## 13. Failure conditions (when to revisit)

If after 90 days of execution:

- **Instagram follower growth < 100/week** → reset creative strategy, A/B test 3 different formats
- **TikTok views avg < 500/video** → algorithm not picking us up; try different sounds + posting times
- **Cost per signup > ₦10,000 on any paid channel** → kill that channel, redirect budget
- **Trial → paid conversion < 5%** → product issue, not marketing; pause paid spend to fix
